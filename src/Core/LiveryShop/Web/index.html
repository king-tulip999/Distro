        How it works

        1. Load your car's template (Car tab). Its wireframe shows where each panel is.
        2. Pick team colors (left). Shapes in team colors change together.
        3. Add shapes, text, numbers and logos (left), then drag them onto the panels. Click a shape to change it in the little toolbar above it, or in the Layer tab.
        4. Mirror copy puts the same thing on the other side of the car.
        5. Export (top right), then put the files in your iRacing paint folder. Or use Live sync in the Export tab to skip that step.
