## <img src="screenshots/velocita-icon.png" width="28" alt=""> Velocita

> For racing in iRacing: overlays, your stats and the tools around the sim.

### iRacing Overlay

20 widgets: relative, standings, fuel and strategy, delta, radar, track map, inputs, tires, weather and more. Drag them around in the car with `Ctrl+Alt+E`, save layouts per car or track, add any widget to OBS as a browser source, or show them in your VR headset.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pit exit** | Where a stop right now would put you, learned per track and car |
| **Strategy** | Fuel saving to skip a stop |
| **Track notes** | Notes per corner, track limits and a race result card |
| **VR** | Any widget in your headset through SteamVR or OpenXR, fixed in the cockpit or following your head, placed per widget or matched to the monitor |
| **Widget editor** | Click any part of a widget to change it, drag its parts and columns into your own order, and pick when it shows |
| **Night mode** | Black widgets in one color for night racing, switched on at sunset in iRacing or by keybind |
| **Alerts** | Fuel, pit speed, traffic, flags, incidents, track limits and weather, each with its own threshold |
| **Fuel** | Your reserve, margin and pit stop time, with every number's working shown |

### Session Stats

Your iRacing history, kept on your PC: result cards, ratings, personal bests, and lap-by-lap telemetry comparison.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Lap analysis** | Compare laps on speed, throttle, brake, steering and gear, see which corners cost the most time, then replay them side by side on the track with live inputs for every car |
| **Overview** | Your season at a glance: race-by-race results, rating changes, recent sessions, when you drive, and your cars and tracks |
| **Each session** | Finish, pace, consistency, conditions, every lap and sector, tire wear per run, and your progress at that track |

### iRacing tools

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Setup Manager** | Every setup sorted by car and class, with new downloads picked up |
| **FOV calculator** | Single or triple screens, compared side by side and written straight into iRacing |
| **iRacing settings** | Every iRacing setting in plain language, searchable, with what you changed from the defaults and one-click undo |
| **iRacing keybinds** | Every in-sim keybind per profile, with what's set, missing or changed |
| **Trading Paints and Garage 61** | Start them with Velocita or when iRacing starts, and see your own paint on car pictures across the app |

### Livery Shop (beta)

Paint your iRacing car, helmet and suit, saved straight into iRacing. Off unless you turn it on.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Painting** | Shapes, stripes, numbers, text, logos, brush, eraser and pen, over iRacing's car template, with team colors that update everything painted in them |
| **Import** | PNG, JPG, SVG, TGA or a Photoshop file with every layer kept, by button, drag and drop or Ctrl+V |
| **Versions** | Save, rename, duplicate, export and import versions of a design, each a file you can open from Velocita |
| **Live preview** | The car drawn by iRacing, updated each time your paint saves |

### GRR League

Your league's next race, standings and results, if you race with GRR. Off unless you turn it on.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Every series** | A countdown to the next race, the schedule, your championship position and season form, the title fight and the latest results |

### Light on your PC

Measured with all six apps running side by side in the same live iRacing session for 10 minutes, each showing comparable standings or relative widgets. Every process an app starts is counted. Lower is better.

## <img src="screenshots/tecnica-icon.png" width="28" alt=""> Tecnica

> For setting up the PC: screens, windows, fans and what your hardware is doing.

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Displays** | Monitors, resolution, HDR, wallpapers and audio saved as setups. Switch from desk to rig with one keybind |
| **Display setups** | Layout, HDR, taskbar, audio devices, volume and a wallpaper per monitor, edited right under the setup's preview |
| **Window placement** | Any program at an exact size and spot, borderless, applied the moment it starts. Great for stretching a game across triples |
| **Placement settings** | Size, position, borders, a start delay and a keybind, with a picture of where the window lands |
| **Performance Overlay** | FPS, 1% lows, temperatures and usage over your game, in whatever order you like |
| **Fan Control** | Curves, per-fan limits, presets and a failsafe that hands the fans back to the BIOS if anything goes wrong |
| **Cooling test** | Measures how much each fan group actually lowers your temperatures and suggests quiet, balanced or cool curves |

## Getting started

1. **[Download](https://github.com/king-tulip999/Distro/releases/latest)** `Velocita.exe`, `Tecnica.exe` or both.
2. Run it. Windows will say it protected your PC: click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Pick a few options on the setup screen and click **Get started**. If it runs from Downloads, the setup screen offers to move it to its own install folder.

> [!TIP]
> Tecnica asks for administrator rights (sensors, fans and displays need them). Velocita doesn't.

> [!NOTE]
> Coming from Pit-Box? The first start copies your old settings, layouts and stats over. The old folder is left as it was.

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Why does Windows warn me?</b></summary>
<br>

The exes aren't code-signed (certificates cost money every year). To check your copy, compare `Get-FileHash Velocita.exe` in PowerShell with the `.sha256` file on the release page. The built-in updater checks every download's signature before installing it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it safe with anti-cheat?</b></summary>
<br>

On a monitor nothing is injected into games: the overlays are separate click-through windows. They show over borderless and windowed games, not exclusive fullscreen.

In VR, widgets are SteamVR overlays when SteamVR is running. With any other OpenXR runtime, Velocita registers a standard OpenXR API layer that iRacing loads to draw them. The layer is only registered while **VR widgets** is on, and only acts in iRacing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What is PawnIO?</b></summary>
<br>

An optional free, signed driver Tecnica can use for CPU temperatures and motherboard fans (Settings → Hardware access → **Install PawnIO**). Without it everything else still works. Velocita never uses it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What does it send online?</b></summary>
<br>

An update check against GitHub releases (if you leave it on), a small ping for Tecnica's network readout, the GRR feed if you show that page, and iRacing's car templates when you load one in the Livery Shop. No accounts, no telemetry. Your stats stay on your PC.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What does it change on my PC?</b></summary>
<br>

Only what you set up. Velocita backs up iRacing files before changing them. Tecnica hands the fans back to the BIOS when it quits. If Tecnica is force-killed, fans stay at their last speed until it restarts or the PC reboots.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do updates work?</b></summary>
<br>

Each app checks for updates in Settings → Application → About and updates. **Install and restart** downloads, verifies and swaps the exe. Your settings are kept.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I uninstall?</b></summary>
<br>

1. Turn off **Start with Windows** in each app.
2. Quit from the tray (in Tecnica, choose **Give fans back and quit** if fan control is on).
3. Delete the exes and `%APPDATA%\Velocita` / `%APPDATA%\Tecnica`.
4. If you installed PawnIO, remove it from Windows Settings → Apps.
</details>
